Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

SeventhBit ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use WakeMeApp (the "App").

WakeMeApp is an alarm application designed to help users manage and schedule alarms on their devices.

1. Information We Collect

WakeMeApp does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data through its normal functionality.

We do not currently use advertising services, analytics services, or third-party tracking SDKs in the App.

2. Alarm Information

Information you enter to configure alarms, such as alarm times and preferences, is used to provide the App's core functionality.

This information is handled on your device and is not transmitted to SeventhBit as part of the App's normal operation.

3. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

WakeMeApp does not automatically send crash reports or diagnostic logs to SeventhBit.

If the App encounters an error, you may have the option to manually share a crash report or diagnostic log with us. Sharing is entirely optional and requires an action from you.

Depending on the error and the information included in the diagnostic log, a report may contain technical details such as device information, operating system information, application version, error messages, and diagnostic data.

When you choose to share a report, it is transferred using the sharing method you select. The selected email or sharing service may process the information according to its own privacy policy.

We use voluntarily submitted crash reports only to investigate technical problems, diagnose errors, and improve the stability and reliability of WakeMeApp.

4. Google Play Billing and PRO Features

WakeMeApp may offer optional premium features ("PRO Features") through subscriptions and one-time purchases. These purchases are processed using Google Play's Billing system.

4.1 Payment Processing

When you purchase a subscription or a one-time PRO feature, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Your payment information, including payment card details, is collected and processed by Google according to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

SeventhBit does not directly collect, access, or store your full credit card number or other complete payment credentials through WakeMeApp.

4.2 Purchase Information

In connection with purchases, Google Play may provide SeventhBit with certain transaction-related information necessary to verify purchases, activate PRO Features, manage subscriptions, process refunds, and provide customer support.

Depending on the billing integration, this information may include:

  • Purchase status and transaction identifiers.
  • Purchased product or subscription information.
  • Purchase date and subscription expiration information.
  • Purchase verification information, such as purchase tokens.
  • Refund, cancellation, or subscription status information.

We use this information only for purchase-related purposes, including verifying that a purchase is valid and ensuring that users receive the PRO Features they have purchased.

4.3 Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, it may automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless you cancel it through Google Play before the renewal date.

Subscription prices, billing periods, renewal conditions, and other applicable terms are displayed during the purchase process.

You can manage or cancel your subscriptions through your Google Play account and its subscription management settings.

4.4 One-Time Purchases

WakeMeApp may also offer one-time purchases that unlock specific PRO Features. These purchases are processed through Google Play and provide the corresponding access according to the description displayed at the time of purchase.

4.5 Refunds and Cancellations

Purchases, refunds, and subscription cancellations are subject to the applicable Google Play policies and purchase terms.

SeventhBit may receive transaction status updates from Google Play when necessary to maintain or revoke access to purchased PRO Features.

4.6 Google's Privacy Policy

Google processes payment and transaction information independently under its own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review Google's privacy information:

Google Privacy Policy

Additional information about Google Play purchases and subscriptions is available at:

Google Play Help – Subscriptions

5. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily shared with us may be used to:

  • Investigate and fix application crashes and errors.
  • Improve the stability and performance of WakeMeApp.
  • Provide technical support when requested.
  • Verify purchases and provide access to PRO Features, if available.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information is shared with third parties only when necessary to provide a service you choose to use, such as an email provider or sharing application used to send a crash report, or Google Play in connection with purchases.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Alarm configuration information is handled locally on your device.

If you voluntarily send us a crash report, we retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the reported issue, provide support, and maintain the security and reliability of the App, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Purchase-related information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage purchases, provide access to PRO Features, comply with legal obligations, and resolve transaction-related issues.

You can contact us to request deletion of a crash report or other information you have voluntarily submitted, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information that users voluntarily provide to us against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

WakeMeApp is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection authority.

Since WakeMeApp does not offer user accounts, there is currently no account registration or account deletion process. You may contact us directly regarding information you have voluntarily shared with us.

11. International Data Transfers

If you voluntarily share information with us or purchase PRO Features, certain information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google, in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required by applicable data protection laws, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
